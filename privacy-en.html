Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Samha (“Samha,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect personal data when you access or use the Samha mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and handling your personal data in accordance with:

  • The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
  • Applicable implementing regulations
  • International data protection and security best practices

By accessing or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of Application

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Samha application, including customers booking beauty services and any individuals interacting with our platform.

It does not apply to third-party services that may be linked within the app. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal data necessary to provide, operate, secure, and improve our Service.

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Booking details and appointment history
  • Beauty preferences
  • Optional profile photos
  • Messages, reviews, and customer support communications

3.2 Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through certified third-party payment providers (including EDFAPAY).

We do not store or process full credit or debit card numbers on our servers.

3.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • Unique device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Log data (access times, pages viewed, actions taken)
  • App usage analytics
  • Approximate or precise location data (only with your explicit permission)

Location data is used solely to display nearby salons and improve user experience.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Your consent
  • Performance of a contract (e.g., booking services)
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate business interests (such as fraud prevention and service improvement), provided such interests do not override your rights

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Facilitate bookings between users and salons
  • Process transactions
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Personalize user experience
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or misuse
  • Improve app performance and features
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements

We do not use your personal data for unauthorized marketing purposes.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We may share personal data only in the following circumstances:

6.1 With Partner Salons

We share necessary booking details with salons to enable appointment fulfillment.

6.2 With Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers that assist us in operating the Service, including:

  • Payment processors
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Customer support platforms

All service providers are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality and data security.

6.3 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose personal data if required by:

  • Saudi laws or regulations
  • Court orders
  • Government authorities

6.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale, personal data may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to confidentiality obligations.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular system monitoring and updates
  • Hosting within secure data centers compliant with Saudi regulatory requirements

Despite our efforts, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service
  • Fulfill contractual obligations
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements (including financial record retention obligations under Saudi law, typically 5–10 years)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

When data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymized.

9. Your Rights Under PDPL

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request data portability where applicable

To exercise your rights, please contact: privacy@samhaapp.com

We may request identity verification before processing certain requests.

10. International Data Transfers

Personal data is primarily stored within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

If data is transferred outside Saudi Arabia, such transfers will comply with PDPL requirements and include appropriate legal safeguards to ensure adequate protection.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has provided personal data, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

12. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain session functionality
  • Remember user preferences
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Improve Service performance

Users may manage certain tracking preferences through device or browser settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time.

If material changes are made, we will notify users through the application or other appropriate means. Continued use of the Service after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
